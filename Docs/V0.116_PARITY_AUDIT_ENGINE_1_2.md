## Parity conclusion

`ZETAGOLD/main` is still the v0.106 execution baseline. Therefore v0.116 is **not yet a byte-for-byte behavioral baseline inside ZETAGOLD/main**. The refactor must not silently treat v0.106 as the definitive behavior reference.

The correct sequence is:

1. Preserve v0.116 as the behavioral reference.
2. Classify every economic action by responsibility.
3. Keep low-level broker execution in Core.
4. Keep observers/telemetry/UI outside the economic engines.
5. Consolidate economic responsibilities into Engine 1 and Engine 2 without redesigning rules.
6. Only then remove compatibility shims.

## Responsibility map

### Engine 1 — Lifecycle & Recovery

Owns the lifecycle of the directional basket and additions required to keep/recover it:

- R1 first-cycle admission/seed orchestration.
- R4 single-position realization and re-entry.
- R5 basket realization, including BRX handoff where enabled.
- R7 keep-alive / missing-direction restart.
- Recovery state and recovery-cycle accounting (R10.2).
- Recovery additions and dynamic recovery step (R11).
- Global pending-order trailing associated with the lifecycle.
- Directional BUY/SELL machine orchestration.

The v0.116 Lifecycle contains transactional close results (`NONE/BLOCKED/COMPLETED/PARTIAL/FAILED`) and applies the action contract after economic mutations. fileciteturn53file0

### Engine 2 — Exposure Management

Owns changes whose primary purpose is exposure balancing/reduction:

- R9 activation-driven hedge.
- R10 profit-funded reduction.
- R10 balanced exposure reduction.
- Exposure invariants: reduction must never increase net exposure.

The v0.116 R9 path participates in the action contract and returns transactional results. fileciteturn55file0

The v0.116 R10 path distinguishes completed, partial and failed broker mutations and prevents fall-through after a partial pair reduction. fileciteturn56file0

- `EAGOLD_R10_Reconciliation.mqh` — broker-visible census after a partial execution; no synthetic reconstruction of broker state. fileciteturn60file0

## Atomicity invariants to preserve

1. R1 first admission: both directional seeds or none.
2. Any partial broker mutation: no subsequent economic action in the same tick.
3. Reconciliation precedes new economic execution after a partial mutation.
4. R7 cannot manufacture a new basket while the basket is completely flat.
5. Pending-entry suspension closes entry authority for the current tick.
6. R10 may reduce exposure but may never increase it.
7. Observers and telemetry do not acquire order authority.
8. Core execution remains the only broker mutation primitive.

## Refactor rule

**No trading-rule redesign is authorized by this stage.**

The refactor changes ownership, include topology and responsibility boundaries only. Economic predicates, lot calculations, price calculations, order comments, sequencing and safety gates must remain equivalent to the v0.116 reference until a separately approved behavior change is made.
